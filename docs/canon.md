# Canon vs. invented

Every design decision that claims to come from the novel is listed here with
its source, so it can be checked. Where the canon is silent, that is said.

**Last verified against the [Shadow Slave Wiki](https://shadowslave.fandom.com/)
on 2026-10-12.** Since v0.4.0, also against chapter text (quoted-phrase
searches of chapter hosts) where the wiki was not enough. ✅ = the quoted wording was found on the wiki page named.
⚠️ = plausible, but not found on the wiki pages checked; needs a chapter
reference before anyone relies on it.

## Ours, not canon

The source never fixes these numbers: every lifespan figure, every
`timeScale`, and all stat and DC values. Canon says only that Awakened "have
longer lifespans than humans" ✅, and the exact amount is unknown.

| Rank | Common name | Lifespan (invented) | timeScale (invented) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dormant | Sleeper | 85 | ×1 |
| Awakened | Awakened | 140 | ×12 |
| Ascended | Master | 320 | ×34 |
| Transcendent | Saint | 900 | ×90 |
| Supreme | Sovereign | 6 000 | ×520 |
| Sacred | Sacred | 40 000 | ×1 200 |
| Divine | Divine | unbounded | ×6 000 |

Also invented, and worth being explicit about because each one *looks* like
it might be canon:

- **How a Divine Aspect comes about.** Canon says it exists and names its
  holders. It never says how. The gate here (an extraordinary appraisal plus a
  rare draw, deliberately *not* gated on lineage) is invented.
- **The four invented divine lineages** (the Salt Queen, the Long Sleeper,
  the Cartographer, the Unmade Smith) were removed in v0.4.0 (issue #4).
- **How a player can carry the Sun, Heart, Shadow or Weaver lineage.** In
  canon each belongs to one named character or a dead line. The game frames
  them as an unknown branch (Sun, Heart), a trace that arrived nobody knows
  how (Shadow), or a loose Weave that devours your own lineage (Weaver,
  never inherited at birth because the gods forbade the daemons offspring).
  The player is never Nephis, Asterion or Sunny. All of their rates are
  ours; see balance.md.
- **The "A Legacy Clan" lineage.** Canon has lesser Legacy Clans (Aegis
  Rose, Dagonet, Han Li, Maharana, Pandavar, White Feather...) with
  hereditary Attributes; the player's family is deliberately none of them.
- **What Memories and Echoes do in a fight.** Canon has them summoned and
  commanded; the Weapon +1, Armor soak, Echo d4 and "an Echo dies in your
  place" numbers are ours. Personal trials (`dcSelf`) take none of it.
- **Your organization**: its names, sizes, events and endings. Canon gives
  the templates (the Irregulars, the Lord of Shadows' Citadel, Ravenheart
  growing from a lesser clan's stronghold); none of the organizations a
  player founds is from the novel.
- **The soul-fragment thresholds for each Class.** Calibrated against
  `tools/sim.js`. Fan summaries quote figures in the thousands; a whole skilled
  life in this game banks about 40 fragments, so those numbers would make
  every rung above Beast unreachable.
- **That an ordinary Awakened is capped at two Soul Cores.** Canon says human
  souls "are not meant to have many cores", and that *some* Awakened can form
  more than one. It never states a hard cap. Ours is an inference.
- **Echo ranks and the Essence an Echo ties up.** Canon gives Echoes no rank
  list and no carry limit.
- **Every Nightmare and solstice region.** All 11 Nightmares and 4 regions are
  original, built on the pattern of the novel's (Forgotten Shore, Dark City,
  Hollow Mountains).

## Fan fiction (fanon)

The repo owner allows fan fiction as a source of ideas when chapter text is
out of reach. The rule here: fanon can inspire a scenario, but it is never
cited as canon, and anything taken from it is listed under "Ours" in that
pack's page in `docs/content/`. The research for v0.5.0 found almost no
usable fan fiction (two stories, neither adding rules), so nothing in the
game currently rests on it.
