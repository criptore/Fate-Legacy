# Module: Aspect Abilities

**Why.** Every Aspect in the game was a stat block and a line of flavour. Canon
gives an Aspect a ladder of Abilities that the character actually uses, at an
Essence cost. The README listed "Aspect Abilities as real mechanics" under
*Planned*.

## How it plays
Unlocked Abilities appear as buttons above the choices of any event that has
a roll. Tap one to arm it, then pick a choice:

- If the choice rolls, the Ability spends its Essence and adds to that roll.
  The roll line shows it, e.g. `8 + CUN 6 + Banked Coal 1 vs 9`.
- If the choice does not roll, nothing is spent.
- Only one Ability can be used per event.

| Tier | Unlocked | Bonus on the Aspect's own attributes | Otherwise | Essence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dormant | First Nightmare survived (with the Flaw) | +2 | +1 | 1 |
| Awakened | Awakened | +3 | +1 | 1 |
| Ascended | Master | +4 | +1 | 2 |
| Transformation | Saint | +6 | +1 | 4 |

- **Personal trials** (`dcSelf`) take only +1. They measure you, and your
  Aspect is already part of you.
- **Names.** Each of the 14 Aspects has its own four names (Shadow: *Unlit
  Step*, *Second Silhouette*, *Where The Light Isn't*, *The Long Dusk*; Iron:
  *Hard Grain*, *Anvil Stance*, *Unbending*, *Living Bulwark*...). None copies
  a canon character's Ability.
- **Where it shows.** The Status page lists your Abilities, and the end screen
  counts how many times you used them.

**The trade-off.** The Seeds of Nightmare require Essence. An Awakened who
spends it on every roll will never have enough to be offered a Second Seed.
This is intended: canon Abilities consume Essence.

## Canon it follows
| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Dormant Ability from the First Nightmare; Awakened Ability; "Ascension grants a third Aspect Ability"; a Transformation at Transcendence; a Domain at Supreme | Aspect Ability, Category:Master (wiki) |

| "Essence can be used for summoning Memories and Echoes or using advanced Aspect Abilities" | Soul Essence (wiki) |

## Ours
Every name, bonus and cost. Canon gives no numbers for Ability costs.

## Numbers
6000 lives per policy, citadel origin. Each run is a baseline against a
player who arms their best affordable Ability on every dangerous roll while
keeping 40 Essence in reserve:

- **random:** Saint+ goes from 3.0% to 3.1%.
- **skilled:** Saint 18.8% to 17.1%, Sovereign 4.8% to 6.0%.
- **climber:** Sovereign 3.8% to 5.1%, Sacred 0.4% to 1.2%.

A player who spends Essence on every roll with no reserve gets stuck at
Awakened.
