# Sovereign and Sacred events

**Why.** The Supreme chapter had four events and the Sacred chapter two.
That is the same shape that stranded a Saint for nine hundred years in
issue #6, and it made the longest lives in the game the most repetitive.

## Sovereign (Supreme)
- **A Citadel Without A Lord** (recurring, three text variants). An empty
  Citadel and its people: take it by force, defend its people until they
  choose you, or let it go.
- **The Saints Come To Swear.** Accept fealty, refuse it and offer alliance,
  or ask the Saint who outlived two Sovereigns what killed them.
- **Godgrave.** The front of the war between Domains runs along a dead
  god's bones. Search the Hollows for a lost Citadel, hold the line on the
  god's shoulder, or refuse the war.
- **A Visit To The Waking World.** Your old city, a statue that does not
  look like you, and the families of those who died beside you.

## Sacred
- **The Window.** You cannot go back to Earth. From a thin place at the edge
  of the Dream Realm you can watch it, warn it, or stop looking.
- **The Bones Of A Dead God.** Listen to the last thought still turning in
  Godgrave's skull.
- **Prayers.** People start praying to you.

## The Domain (new mechanic)
A Sovereign's Domain counts **Citadels** (up to 3). Each one adds +1 to
dangerous checks from Supreme up, shown on the roll line as "Domain". It is
listed on the Status page and the end screen. Holding three earns
*A Great Citadel*, and the Citadel offer stops coming.

## Ours
Every event, the numbers of the Domain bonus, and the Citadel cap of three.
None of the named canon Sovereigns appears.

## Numbers
1500 Sovereign lives played at random from typical Sovereign stats:
- **Before:** median age 2,994 years. Deaths: Nightmare 29%, Gate 25%,
  Chain 24%, age 21%.
- **After:** median age 4,077 years. Deaths: age 36%, Nightmare 27%,
  Chain 24%, Gate 13%. The average Domain at death is 1.35 Citadels.

Living longer at the top is the canon point of a Domain.
