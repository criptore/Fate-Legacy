# Third Nightmare: The Fleet That Never Lands

A third Third Nightmare, drawn at random alongside the Assembly of Mouths and
the Quiet Continent.

**The story.** The Spell makes you a navigator's mate on the flagship of ten
thousand ships that have been at sea for a thousand years, sailing for a
promised shore. A herd of Great-ranked sea creatures, the Shepherd, turns
back any ship that strays. Eleven years in, you learn the truth: the fleet is
not sailing toward anything. It is fleeing a Defiled shore hidden inside a
storm, and the storm is now faster than the fleet.

**Three nodes.**
1. Read the charts, find the other challengers, or dive to see the Shepherd.
2. Eleven years later: sail into the storm, tell the ten thousand captains
   the truth, or hunt a young Shepherd (Echo: *The Shepherd's Calf*).
3. Four endings:
   - lead the fleet into the storm and cleanse the shore;
   - kill the eldest Shepherd and let the fleet choose;
   - help a silent challenger finish a true chart of a clean coast;
   - try to sail away alone. The sea of a Nightmare has no edge, so the only
     way to survive this is to turn back and end it.

On a successful ending the Saint screen opens with a line saying the Seed
returned you to the moment you entered, as canon describes for the Tomb of
Ariel.

## Ours
The fleet, the *Long Patience*, the Shepherd herd, the storm and the shore.
*The Shoreless Chart* (Utility Memory), *The Shepherd's Calf* (Echo, Ascended
rank and 4 Essence like the other Fallen-born Echo) and the *Made Landfall*
achievement are new. The council check is deliberately hard (DC 44 against an
average Willpower of about 39 at a Third Seed).

## Numbers
At the average stats of a life that reaches the Third Seed (sampled from
random play), 4000 runs with random choices: 48% become Saints and 52% die.
The two older Third Nightmares kill 60–66% under the same test. Time inside
the Seed does not age you, matching the canon return to the moment of entry.
