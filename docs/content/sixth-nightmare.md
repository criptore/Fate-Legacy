# The Sixth Nightmare, and a Divine life

**Why.** The game stopped at Sacred: "Look for the Sixth" set a flag and led
nowhere, and the Divine rank had no events. A life that somehow reached it
would drift until the game killed it with "Nothing came for you".

**The offer (`sx_offer`, Sacred).** Once you have chosen to look for the
Sixth and are strong enough, the last Seed appears, at the bottom of the
Dream Realm. You can enter, wait, or refuse for good. The offer recurs until
you take it or refuse it, so "not yet" never strands the life.

**The Nightmare (`sx_1` to `sx_resolve`).** A world with one law of
existence missing: nothing in it ever ends. A sun that never sets, a wave that
never breaks, a woman who has been dying for ten thousand years with
everyone she loved unable to grieve.
1. Understand what an ending is for, or prove by force that one can happen.
2. Become the law: a gentle one (things end when they are finished) or an
   exact one (everything ends in its time, without exception).
3. Become Divine. The *Divine* achievement goes first on the end screen.

**A Divine life (`dv_first`, `dv_ages`).** What a god is for: watch over
those still climbing, wander the edges of the Dream Realm, or be still. Then
the ages pass, and at any point you may weave yourself fully into the law
and end the life. That is the only way a Divine life ends, and it earns
*Author of a Law*. More Divine events live in the divine-era pack.

## Canon it follows
| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| The Sixth Nightmare is "the final Nightmare"; conquering it makes one Divine | Paths of Ascension (wiki) |
| Divine is "a true god whose essence encompasses all of existence, being woven into the universal laws" | wiki |
| The Divine rank has no human name "because no one has reached it" | wiki |
| A Nightmare ends in conquest or death | Repo owner's ruling; docs/canon.md |

Canon also hints at a Seventh Nightmare and at what the Spell was really for.
That is a heavy late-story spoiler, and this pack deliberately does not touch
it.

## Ours
Everything inside the Sixth Nightmare, the offer's location, and the Divine
events and ending. The idea of the Sixth as a trial against the laws
themselves came out of the lore research as a design suggestion; it is not
canon.

## Numbers
6000 runs from the entry at stats around 85–92, random choices: 10% become
Divine and 90% die. In 400 Sacred lives played at random, every life that
reached Divine ended by weaving itself into the law.
