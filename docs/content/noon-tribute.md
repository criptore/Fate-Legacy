# Second Nightmare: The Noon Tribute

A fourth Second Nightmare, drawn at random alongside the Lantern March, the
Iron Orchard and the Court of Uncounted Debts.

**The story.** The Spell makes you a soldier of Varrow, a walled city besieged
for three hundred years by a circle of Fallen-ranked walkers. Every day at
noon the gate opens and one citizen, chosen by lot, is handed to the
Corrupted thing at the centre. The city's founder, the Keeper of Hours, made
that bargain, and the city has kept it ever since. Five other challengers
woke inside the walls with you.

**Three nodes.**
1. Waking: gather the other challengers, watch the siege lines, or volunteer
   for the lottery.
2. Twenty days later: search the clock tower (find the pact), fight a siege
   walker at night (Echo: *A Warden of the Outer Wall*), or listen to the
   citizens.
3. The last noon. There are four ways to end it, as canon's "there is no
   right or wrong way" requires:
   - tear up the pact (needs the pact; the Keeper draws her own name);
   - walk out and kill the thing at the centre;
   - lead the whole city out (needs allies or the citizens' trust);
   - try to slip out unseen. A Nightmare has no outside, so this is the
     most dangerous path, and the only way to survive it is to turn round
     and end the Nightmare from behind.

## Ours
Varrow, the Keeper of Hours, the tribute, the siege and every name in it.
*The Noon Bell's Tongue* (Weapon Memory) and *A Warden of the Outer Wall*
(Echo) are new catalogue entries.

## Numbers
The decisive checks are personal trials (`dcSelf`), so a high stat does not
make them free. In 4000 test runs from the entry node at realistic Awakened
stats, with random choices: 76–78% ascend and 22–24% die. With the two
sturdiest options (the pact, or fighting the centre) the death chance is 10%.
For comparison, the three older Second Nightmares kill fewer than 2% of
challengers at the same stats.
