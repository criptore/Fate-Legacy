# The Divine era

More to do with forever. The Sixth Nightmare pack makes Divine reachable and
gives it two events and one ending (weaving yourself into the law). This
pack adds variety and a second ending.

**Needs the Sixth Nightmare pack.** On its own, nothing can reach the Divine
chapter, so `validate.js` warns that the chapter is never entered. With both
packs merged the warning disappears.

## Events
- **A First Nightmare, From Outside** (recurring, three variants). A
  seventeen-year-old is about to face their First Nightmare. You may stand
  beside them, unseen, never helping, for the whole of it. That earns
  *Someone Was Watching*.
- **A Small Life.** Fold yourself down to the size of a person and live one
  ordinary eighty-year life in a river town.
- **A Lesser God Asks.** A Sacred who found the Sixth Seed asks whether she
  should go in.
- **The Edge.** The Dream Realm has an edge, and for the first time in an age
  you do not know what is beyond something. Stepping over is the second way
  a Divine life can end, and it earns *Beyond The Edge*.

## Canon
Canon only says that Divine is "a true god ... woven into the universal
laws" and that no human has reached it. Everything here is ours, kept small
and human on purpose. The novel's late-story revelations about the Spell and
what lies past the last Nightmare are deliberately left alone.

## Numbers
With the Sixth Nightmare pack merged locally, 300 Divine lives played at
random saw every event, and ended 59% by weaving into the law and 41% by
going beyond the edge. `validate.js` on the combination: 101 events, 0
errors, 0 warnings.
