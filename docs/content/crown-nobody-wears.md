# Fourth Nightmare: The Crown Nobody Wears

A second Fourth Nightmare, drawn at random alongside the Quiet Between Stars.

**The story.** You are alone in a continent-sized kingdom where every city faces
an empty throne on a mountain. Seven Cursed-ranked Regents rule in its name.
A century in, you learn that the Regents were one person: a challenger who,
nine thousand years ago, tried to make the whole land an extension of their
own will. Their soul tore into seven pieces, and each piece went on ruling a
seventh of the land.

**Two nodes.**
1. Sit on the throne on the first day, walk the land for twenty years, or
   challenge a Regent.
2. The decisive trial, with three ways to become the land's Domain:
   - by force and will, as the first challenger tried (the Regents fold back
     into you);
   - through the people, city by city (needs to have walked the land or sat
     the throne);
   - by refusing the crown and giving the throne to the people, who then ask
     you to keep them. This is the hardest, and it earns *Crowned by Consent*.

| A Nightmare ends in conquest or death | Repo owner's ruling; docs/canon.md |

Canon gives no account of how any Fourth Nightmare is structured, so the
structure is ours. The Regent who tore is the scenario's warning about the
canon idea that a Domain is a soul stretched over territory and people.

## Ours
The kingdom, the throne, the seven Regents and their maker. *The Eighth Seal*
(Supreme-rank Charm) and *Crowned by Consent* are new.

## Numbers
At Saint stats around 45–52, 5000 runs from the entry node with random
choices: 28% become Sovereigns and 72% die. The Quiet Between Stars gives 23%
and 77% under the same test.
