# Solstice region: The Hanging Forest

A fifth region where the winter solstice can put a Sleeper down, drawn at
random alongside the Saltglass Flats, the Ledger, the Ruined Belfry and the
Harvest Vaults.

**The place.** A forest that grows downward from a ceiling of stone. Sleepers
wake in the crowns of upside-down trees, with the ground blue with distance
below them. Pale silk the forest grows between its branches carries
long-legged things that hunt whatever shakes it. At the bottom, sixty feet
below the lowest branches, a meadow full of Awakened-ranked things that
cannot climb surrounds the Gateway.

**Three nodes.**
1. Gather the others, climb alone, or watch how the forest moves (learn to
   climb on bark, never on silk).
2. A hundred-foot gap: cross on the silk (*Rootsilk Line*, a Memory), kill
   the crawler on it (*A Canopy Crawler*, an Echo), or take the long way over
   the bark if you learned to.
3. The meadow: drop and run, lower everyone on a silk line beside the
   Gateway, or let someone else drop first.

## Canon it follows
| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Sleepers are taken to a region of the Dream Realm on the winter solstice and become Awakened only by returning through a Gateway | Paths of Ascension (wiki) |
| A region has one landscape, one dominant hazard and a Gateway a long way from where Sleepers land | Forgotten Shore / Dark City template (wiki) |
| A Sleeper's region holds Dormant and Awakened creatures, not Death Zone ranks | Creature ranks; Death Zones hold Great, Cursed and Unholy (chapter text) |

## Ours
The forest, its silk, the crawlers and the meadow. *Rootsilk Line*,
*A Canopy Crawler* and the *All The Way Down* achievement.

## Numbers
8000 runs from the entry at Sleeper stats (6–13), random choices: 87% reach
the Gateway, 13% die. The Ruined Belfry and the Saltglass Flats give 11–13%
under the same test.
