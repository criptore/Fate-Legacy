# Fate Legacy

**▶ Play it: https://criptore.github.io/Fate-Legacy/** (free, in your browser, works on phone)

A narrative life-sim set in the world of **Shadow Slave**. One game is one
whole life: the week the Nightmare Spell infects you, your First Nightmare,
and every rank you manage to climb after it, until something finally stops you.

---

## What this is

**Fate Legacy is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project** made for readers
of Shadow Slave. It is not written or endorsed by GuiltyThree, and it makes
no money: no ads, no donations, no premium tier, none planned, ever. It exists
to turn the book's Soul Rank ladder into a fun, replayable interactive story,
free for anyone to play.

The source is open for the same reason. If you enjoy the setting, take the
code and run with it.

If GuiltyThree or a rights holder would rather this didn't exist in this form,
opening an issue is enough. It will be taken down without argument.

---

## How it plays

- **Pick an Origin.** The Outskirts, a Citadel Ward or a Great Clan. It is
  also the difficulty setting.
- **Survive your First Nightmare.** A different one is drawn every life. Make
  it out and the Spell grants you an Aspect, and a Flaw shaped by how you
  played it.
- **Climb.** Sleeper, Awakened, Master, Saint and beyond: each rank gives you
  centuries more, and each one makes the world harder on you. Gates open,
  Seeds grow, and every choice can be a dice roll against your attributes.
- **Die, and begin again.** Most lives end early. That is part of the story.
  The end screen shows how far you got and what you achieved.

A short guide opens the first time you play, and stays available from the
home screen and the in-game toolbar.

## Features

- The full Soul Rank ladder, from Aspirant to Divine
- 3 Origins, 14 Aspects, 14 Flaws and 10 Lineages, including all seven
  divine lineages of the novel (the Weaver's can only be gained in play, and
  is the rarest), for thousands of possible characters
- Choices that only appear for your own Aspect or Flaw
- 11 hand-written Nightmares and 4 solstice regions, all original and built
  inside the novel's rules
- Memories and Echoes that help in a fight: Weapons and Armor count on
  dangerous checks, and an Echo fights beside you and can die in your place
- Found a company, an order or a landing zone, and run it: recruits,
  rival clans, members who become Lost, and what became of it after you
- Soul Cores, Tether, Gate duty and a world that keeps getting more
  dangerous
- Achievements and a full life summary on the end screen
- Autosave, offline play (install it from your browser's "Add to Home
  Screen"), and a day mode

## Feedback

**Found a bug, or have thoughts after a run?** The end screen has
*Report a bug* and *Leave feedback* buttons. They open a GitHub issue
pre-filled with your run's details (you need a free GitHub account). That is
the whole feedback loop, and it is how the game improves.
